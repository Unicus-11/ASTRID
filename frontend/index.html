What We Do

ASTRID combines multi-agent intelligence, heterogeneous sensing, physics-informed estimation, and adaptive control to deliver a smarter, real-time view of urban traffic.

01

Multi-Agent Traffic Intelligence

Coordinate intelligent agents across traffic signals and road approaches to reason about local congestion while responding to the behaviour of the wider traffic network.

02

Hybrid Traffic State Estimation

Fuse roadside cameras, sparse GPS probe vehicles, simulation data, and historical observations to estimate traffic states that cannot be directly observed.

03

Physics-Informed Prediction

Incorporate traffic-flow theory, conservation laws, LWR dynamics, shockwaves, and queue behaviour to make predictions consistent with how traffic actually evolves.

04

Adaptive Signal Control

Use reconstructed traffic conditions with intelligent control strategies such as Max-Pressure to adapt signal decisions, reduce queue growth, and improve junction performance.
